## Description

Idle surfaces a brief, research-backed activity every time you're waiting for an AI response — instead of anxiously refreshing the page or reaching for your phone.

**How it works**
When Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or Perplexity begins generating a response, Idle automatically shows a small floating panel with a suggested activity matched to your wait time. When the response arrives, the panel quietly fades away.

**Activity categories**
- Physical Reset — eye rest, shoulder rolls, breathing, stretching (0–60 second waits)
- Context Preserving — re-read your prompt, name an assumption, picture the expected output (1–3 minute waits)
- Write Only — capture the follow-up question, note a decision, draft the handoff note (3–5 minute waits)
- Diffuse — look out a window, close your eyes, let your mind wander (5+ minute waits)

**Designed for focus, not engagement**
No streaks. No scores. No notifications. Activities rotate so you don't see the same suggestion twice. The panel fades away automatically when the response arrives. Everything stays local — nothing leaves your browser.

**Privacy**
All data lives in your browser (IndexedDB). No telemetry, no accounts, no network calls. Prompt capture is opt-in and session-scoped — cleared when you close the tab.

**Supported sites**
- claude.ai
- chatgpt.com
- gemini.google.com
- perplexity.ai
