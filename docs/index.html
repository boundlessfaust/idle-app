idle

Brain breaks for AI wait times

Idle surfaces a brief, research-backed activity every time you wait for an AI response — instead of anxiously refreshing or picking up your phone. When the response arrives, the panel quietly fades away.

Get Idle for Chrome

Activities matched to your wait time

  • Physical Reset 0–60 s Eye rest, shoulder rolls, slow breathing, stretching
  • Context Preserving 1–3 min Re-read your prompt, name an assumption, picture the expected output
  • Write Only 3–5 min Draft the follow-up, note a decision, write the handoff line
  • Diffuse 5 min+ Look out a window, close your eyes, let your mind wander
No telemetry. No accounts. Everything stays in your browser. Idle makes zero network requests. All data is stored locally in IndexedDB and never leaves your device. Prompt capture is opt-in and session-scoped only.
